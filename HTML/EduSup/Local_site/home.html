About

EduSup is a Learning Management System built by INDP2 students of SUP'COM. It allows students to register, consult existing courses, propose workshops and training, and request assistance in carrying out their projects.

Courses

EduSup offers a growing catalog of courses covering web development fundamentals, taught step by step with videos, documents and hands-on examples.
